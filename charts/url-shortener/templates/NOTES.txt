url-shortener is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Reach the API:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "url-shortener.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then:
  curl -s -X POST localhost:8080/shorten -d '{"url":"https://go.dev"}'

The Redis PVC is kept after `helm uninstall`. Delete it with:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} delete pvc data-{{ include "url-shortener.redisName" . }}-0
